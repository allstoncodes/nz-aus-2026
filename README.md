## Day-by-Day

### Pre-Trip: May 14–15 (SFO → Airborne → AKL)

**May 14 (Thu) — Departure Day:** Pack up in SF, head to SFO by 8pm for UA 917's 22:45 departure. The flight is 13h25m nonstop over the Pacific — longest haul of the trip. Allston's on paid economy, Donnie on the UA buddy pass (standby UA 917). If standby doesn't clear, Donnie has backup options on other UA SFO→AKL departures that week.

**May 15 (Fri) — Entire day in-flight:** You'll cross the international date line and "skip" Friday entirely. Watch movies, sleep, hydrate. Arrive rested — Day 1 in Auckland is a soft landing day.

---

### Auckland, NZ (May 16–18)

**Day 1 · Sat May 16 — Arrival:** Touch down at AKL 7:10am after the redeye. Customs, Skybus to CBD (~45min, ~$25 NZD). Hotel bag drop at Hilton Auckland on Princes Wharf (check-in 3pm). Keep Day 1 intentionally low-key — brunch at Wynyard Quarter, walk the waterfront, nap. Dinner at MASU by Nic Watt on Federal St for a proper Auckland welcome: Japanese robata, outstanding wagyu, lively CBD atmosphere. Book ahead via SevenRooms.

**Day 2 · Sun May 17 — Full Auckland:** Up at 6am for Mount Eden sunrise — Uber up (~15min), 360° crater rim view before the city wakes. Auckland War Memorial Museum 10am, Māori cultural show / Haka at 11am (don't miss this). UV rest 1–3pm. Drive or Uber to Piha Beach for 4:30pm golden hour — the black volcanic sand, Lion Rock, west-facing Tasman Sea light. Drive back, dinner at Bistro Saine (new 2026 opening, French-Mediterranean by a Korean-born NZ chef) or Forest in Mt Eden (plant-based, Metro Supreme Restaurant of the Year).

**Day 3 · Mon May 18 — Hobbiton:** Coach pickup from Hilton Auckland at 7:30am by GreatSights NZ (~NZD $150–200pp all-in including tour). 2h15m drive south to Hobbiton Movie Set in Matamata. Even if you're not a LOTR fanatic, the craftsmanship is genuinely insane — it feels like a fairytale, and the level of physical detail you don't see at US theme parks. Green Dragon Inn for a pint. Coach returns you to Auckland by 2:30pm. Afternoon free — walk the waterfront, grab a flat white, pack bags. Early dinner, bed by 9pm — 3:30am wake-up for the MEL flight.

---

### Melbourne, AUS (May 19–21)

**Day 4 · Tue May 19 — Transit + Melbourne Arrival:** 3:30am wake at Hilton Auckland, Uber to airport by 4:30am, QF 152 departs 6:10am and lands MEL 8:20am (2h10m, one timezone gain). SkyBus from airport to CBD (~30min, ~$24 AUD). Bag drop at Hilton South Wharf on the Yarra River. Brunch riverside. Afternoon recovery — the Hosier Lane street art is 10min walk from the hotel, zero effort, perfect for a jet-lagged afternoon wander. Dinner at Flower Drum in Chinatown for Melbourne's most iconic Chinese — the Peking duck is a decade-long institution. 15–20min walk from hotel.

**Day 5 · Wed May 20 — Brighton + Penguin Parade:** Early Uber to Brighton Bathing Boxes for sunrise (~7:40am) — the colorful Victorian-era wooden boxes, north-facing, golden light. Back to CBD for late breakfast. Hit Federation Square and ACMI for the cultural morning. Early afternoon, drive/tour bus east to Phillip Island (~1.5h). Little Penguin Parade at sunset (~5:15pm) — hundreds of the world's smallest penguins waddling out of the ocean to their burrows. Genuinely otherworldly. Both Andrew and Donnie flagged this. Back in Melbourne by 8:30pm for a light dinner near the hotel.

**Day 6 · Thu May 21 — Spa + Ishizuka:** Slow morning, spa session 10am–noon (Sakura Lounge or Bamford Spa at 1 Hotel). Laneway afternoon — Melbourne's coffee culture and street art is best explored at your own pace through Degraves St and the CBD alleys. Then: **Ishizuka** at 5:30pm, early seating. 16-seat kaiseki in a basement entered via an apartment elevator on Bourke St — you won't find a room like this in the US. Email reservation@ishizuka.com.au immediately. This is Thursday (they're open Thu–Mon) so it works. Back to hotel, pack bags for 5am departure.

---

### Sydney, AUS (May 22–25)

**Day 7 · Fri May 22 — Transit + Vivid Night 1:** 5am wake at Hilton South Wharf, JQ 500 departs MEL 6am and lands SYD 7:30am (1h30m). Opal rail from airport to CBD (13min, ~$20 AUD). Bag drop at Hilton Sydney on George St — walking distance to QVB. Royal Botanic Garden walk for the soft morning. Observatory Hill golden hour (4:30–6pm) — best compressed view of the Harbour Bridge + city skyline, warm sandstone light. The Rocks for dinner. Then: **Vivid Sydney opens today.** Walk Circular Quay at night as a preview — the Opera House sails lit up, laser shows over the harbour. Save the kayak for May 24.

**Day 8 · Sat May 23 — Iconic Day:** Pre-dawn Uber to Mrs Macquarie's Chair for 6:45am sunrise — the only spot in Sydney where you get the Opera House and Harbour Bridge in a single frame with harbour water as foreground. Arrive 20 minutes early. Opera House guided tour 9am (book AUD $45). Afternoon rest at hotel, then Uber to Bondi for the Bondi→Coogee coastal walk at 4pm — 6km of clifftop trail, Tamarama, Bronte, Clovelly, Icebergs ocean pool at golden hour. From Coogee, short Uber (~15min) to Sean's in North Bondi for dinner. Sean's has been serving 3-course farm-to-table with crashing waves audible since 1993 — Time Out Sydney Best Special Occasion 2025. Book a week ahead.

**Day 9 · Sun May 24 — Epic Day (Burrawa Climb + Vivid Drone Kayak):** Earliest wake-up of the whole trip. Walk to BridgeClimb at 6am for the dawn Burrawa slot. The Burrawa Climb is the Indigenous version — led by an Eora Nation storyteller who explains Sydney Harbour's history from an Aboriginal perspective while you're 440 feet above the water. 3.5h, ends around 10am. Post-climb brunch. Ferry to Manly (30min from Circular Quay) for the beach and the scenic walkway. Spa afternoon — Venustus for the signature 6-handed massage (3 therapists synchronized). Dinner near The Rocks. Then: **Vivid Drone Show Kayak Tour at 8:30pm** from the Australian National Maritime Museum in Darling Harbour. May 24 is a designated drone show night — drones + light installations over the harbour from a glowing kayak. $135 AUD/pp, 1.5h. Sydney Harbour Kayaks. Book tonight.

**Day 10 · Mon May 25 — Memorial Day Buffer:** The US public holiday means no work. Slow morning, long lunch, maybe one last beach or harbour walk. Pack bags for 7am departure tomorrow.

## Practical Info

### Currency

| | Rate (May 3, 2026) | $100 USD = |
|---|---|---|
| USD → NZD | 1.6949 | NZD $169.49 |
| USD → AUD | 1.39 | AUD $139.00 |

**Cash to carry per person:** NZD $100–120 (~$70 USD) for 3 Auckland days. AUD $150 (~$108 USD) for 7 AU days. Both countries are extremely cashless — this covers markets, tips, and the occasional cash-only spot.

**ATM tip:** Withdraw NZD at AKL airport on arrival (better rate than exchange counters). Withdraw AUD at MEL airport on Day 4.

### Credit Cards

Both countries are among the most cashless in the world. Tap-to-pay (contactless) is universal — even at cafés and markets.

- **Visa / Mastercard:** accepted everywhere
- **Apple Pay:** fully supported at all modern terminals
- **Amex:** great at hotels and restaurants, patchy at smaller spots
- **Capital One / Chase Sapphire Preferred:** no foreign transaction fees — use these for everything else
- **Amex MR cards:** use for hotels and restaurants for 4x Membership Rewards points

### eSIM — Airalo

**Best plan: Oceania Regional** — covers both NZ + AUS in one eSIM (~$4.50+ depending on data). No switching required when crossing from NZ to AUS. Buy via the Airalo iOS app. **Each person needs their own.** Activate before May 14 departure.

Backup: NZ 15-day 10GB for $23.50 USD (Spark network) + AUS 15-day plan separately.

**Day 3 (Hobbiton):** GreatSights NZ coach from hotel — no driving needed.

### UV — Donnie's Accutane Constraint

Southern Hemisphere fall UV is manageable. Melbourne UV ~3, Auckland UV 3–5, Sydney UV ~5 (beach/harbour water reflects up to 25% extra — caution near water). SPF 50+, avoid direct sun 11am–2pm, prioritize early morning and golden hour for outdoor activities. All outdoor blocks in the schedule are planned accordingly.
