Left-Side Driving (NZ leg)

New Zealand drives on the LEFT side of the road — steering wheel is front-right. Pedals are the same order as US. July 10–13 NZ rental leg only.

  • Roundabouts go clockwise — yield to traffic from your right
  • Right turns cross oncoming traffic — the harder turn; use turn lanes
  • Lane drift warning — most common rental incident on unfamiliar-side roads
  • International Driver's Permit (IDP) — required in NZ with a US license
  • Route: AKL → Waitomo → Matamata → Rotorua → AKL · ~5–6h total driving over 3 days

July Packing Notes

July = austral winter. Melbourne and Sydney are mild (8–17°C / 46–62°F). New Zealand (inland/Rotorua area) can be cold at night. Waitomo caves are cool inside year-round.

  • Layers — fleece + light waterproof jacket essential
  • Evening wear — SSO concert (Thu Jul 9) — smart casual
  • Cave / outdoor gear — Waitomo: wear clothes you don't mind getting wet
  • Zorb — swimsuit or quick-dry clothes underneath
