Left-Side Driving (NZ leg)

New Zealand drives on the LEFT side of the road — steering wheel is front-right. Pedals are the same order as US. July 10–13 NZ rental leg only.

  • Roundabouts go clockwise — yield to traffic from your right
  • Right turns cross oncoming traffic — the harder turn; use turn lanes
  • Lane drift warning — most common rental incident on unfamiliar-side roads
  • International Driver's Permit (IDP) — required in NZ with a US license
  • Route: AKL → Hamilton → Waitomo → Matamata/Hobbiton → Rotorua → AKL · ~6–7h total driving over 3 days

Getting Around
  • Melbourne: Uber + DiDi + Bolt. Free CBD trams (Zone 1). Myki card for suburbs. Car likely needed for Phillip Island (Jul 4).
  • Sydney: Uber + Opal card for trains/ferries. Manly ferry from Circular Quay. No car needed in Sydney.
  • New Zealand: Rental car from AKL airport Jul 10. Self-drive all NZ days — AKL → Hamilton (N1) → Waitomo → Matamata/Hobbiton → Rotorua (N2+N3) → AKL airport (Jul 13 AM).
Cards & Payments
  • Visa / Mastercard / Apple Pay — universally accepted in both countries
  • No-FTF cards — use cards with no foreign transaction fees for everything
  • Amex — accepted at hotels and restaurants; less common at small retailers
  • Cash — keep AUD 100–150 and NZD 80–100 for markets and cash-only spots
July Packing Notes

July = austral winter. Melbourne and Sydney are mild (8–17°C / 46–62°F). New Zealand (inland/Rotorua area) can be cold at night. Waitomo caves are cool inside year-round.

  • Layers — fleece + light waterproof jacket essential
  • Evening wear — SSO concert (Thu Jul 9) — smart casual
  • Outdoor gear — walking shoes + warm layers for Hobbiton, coastal walks, Penguin Parade at sunset (Waitomo Glowworm boat tour stays dry)
  • Zorb — swimsuit or quick-dry clothes underneath
