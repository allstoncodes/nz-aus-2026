ATM tip: Withdraw NZD at AKL airport on arrival (Day 1). Withdraw AUD at MEL airport on Day 4. Better rates than currency exchange counters.

Credit Cards & Apple Pay
  • Visa / Mastercard — universally accepted in both countries
  • Apple Pay — fully supported at all modern terminals (tap-to-pay everywhere)
  • Amex — good at hotels + restaurants; less common at small shops
  • Capital One / Chase Sapphire — no foreign transaction fees ✓ use for everything
  • Amex cards — hotels + restaurants for bonus points (4x MR on dining)
  • No-FTF cards — Capital One Venture, Chase Sapphire Preferred
