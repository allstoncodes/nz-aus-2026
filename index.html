⚠ Left-Side Driving (NZ + AU)

Both NZ and Australia drive on the LEFT side of the road — like Japan, UK, Ireland. Steering wheel is on the front-right (driver sits where the US passenger normally sits). Pedals are still in the same order. Donnie is the rental driver — confirm he has the International Driver's License + has practiced or is comfortable.

  • Roundabouts go clockwise — yield to traffic from your right (opposite of US).
  • Right turns cross oncoming traffic — the "harder" turn, opposite of US. Use the right-turn arrow lane.
  • Lane drift is the most common rental incident — drivers tend to drift left when zoning out. Stay focused first 30 min + on long highway stretches.
  • Acclimation block: first 60 min after Hertz pickup Sat 5/16 ~10:00 is low-traffic suburban driving — don't schedule activities before 11:00.
  • Tolls: Northern Gateway (SH1 north of AKL) is electronic-only, but the AKL→Hamilton route (SH1 south) is toll-free.
  • Hertz NZ caveats: +2.53% credit card surcharge (incl GST) on the booking. Diesel/EV adds $0.0874/km Road User Charge — pick a gas Compact SUV (e.g. Kia Seltos petrol) to avoid. Provide flight number UA 917 at pickup or vehicle gets held at domestic terminal. NZ requires foreign license + IDP (Donnie acquiring).
Capital One Venture X — Auto Rental CDW

Allston's Venture X covers rental car damage in NZ + AU as primary insurance (verified from policy guide 2026-05-06). Activates only when (1) entire rental transaction is paid on Venture X AND (2) you decline Hertz's counter CDW/LDW upsell.

  • ✅ Covered: theft + collision damage to your rental, up to vehicles with MSRP-when-new ≤ $75K (Compact SUV well under). Loss-of-use, admin fees, towing.
  • ❌ NOT covered: damage to other drivers' cars or injury (third-party liability), personal belongings, off-road damage, driver-impairment damage. Country exclusions: Israel, Jamaica, Rep of Ireland, Northern Ireland — NZ + AU not excluded.
  • ⚠️ Buy Hertz Liability Insurance Supplement (~$15-20 NZD/day) for the 4-day rental — Cap One CDW does not cover damage you do to other vehicles. ~$60-80 total. Decline CDW + accept LIS.
  • If anything happens: call Cap One Benefit Administrator immediately at 1-800-825-4062 (US) / 1-804-965-8071 (collect from NZ). File at eclaimsline.com. Report within 45 days; claim form within 90 days.
  • Before leaving the rental lot: walk around the car and photograph any existing damage to avoid being charged at return.
